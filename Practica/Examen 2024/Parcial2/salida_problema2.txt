EDO: dy/dx = x*exp(x^2)/y,   y(0) = 1
Solucion exacta: y(x) = exp(x^2/2)
Paso h = 0.1

Metodo propuesto (Adams-Bashforth 2 pasos):
  y[n+1] = y[n] + h/2 * (3*f(x[n],y[n]) - f(x[n-1],y[n-1]))
  y[1] se calcula con Euler: y[1] = y[0] + h*f(x[0],y[0])
